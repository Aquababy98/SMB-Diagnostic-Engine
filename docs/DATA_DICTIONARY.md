### burn_rate
| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | FLOAT |
| Formula | `(cash_outflow - monthly_revenue) / monthly_revenue` |
| Expected range | -1.0 to 2.0 |
| Interpretation | Negative = profitable (spending less than earning) · Zero = break even · Positive = burning cash |
| Risk threshold | Values above 0.10 trigger `cash_flow_flag = 1` |
| Known issues | 480 extreme outlier values between 2.5–8.0 (system export corruption) |
| Example | `0.0423` |

### risk_score
| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Type | FLOAT |
| Range | 0 — 100 |
| Formula | `(burn_rate × 0.50 + churn_rate × 0.30 + (lead_time_days / 60) × 0.20) × 100` |
| Weights | Cash flow 50% · Churn 30% · Lead time 20% — weighted by SMB failure rate research |
| Interpretation | Higher score = higher composite risk. Score of 0 = no measurable risk. Score of 100 = maximum risk across all three dimensions. |
| Example | `18.42` |

## Risk Weight Rationale

The composite `risk_score` weights are derived from US Chamber of Commerce SMB failure research (2025–2026):

| Dimension | Weight | Failure Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Cash flow (burn_rate) | 50% | 82% of SMB failures involve cash flow mismanagement |
| Market fit (churn_rate) | 30% | 42% of SMB failures involve loss of customer relevance |
| Operations (lead_time_days) | 20% | Proxy for labor and productivity gap |
